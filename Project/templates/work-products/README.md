# Work Product templates

* **WP type:** Guide
* **Version:** 0.1.0
* **Date:** 2026-11-12

These forms are adapted from `kuzmin-machine-ng/Project/templates/` for
general R&D use. They do not imply that a document is approved or validated.
Use the [Work Product policy](../../policies/work-products.md) and choose a
template based on the artifact's purpose.

## Use

Copy a form into its intended area, fill only what is known, and remove
inapplicable prompts. Preserve source material and raw data unchanged.
Record unknown fields as TBD with a reason rather than guessing.
